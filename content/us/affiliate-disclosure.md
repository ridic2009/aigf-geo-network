## What an affiliate link is

Some outbound links on this site are affiliate links. If you follow one and sign
up for a paid plan, we may receive a commission from the vendor. You pay the same
price either way.

## What it does not change

Ratings, ranking positions and pros/cons lists are produced by the editorial team
before any commercial discussion. A vendor cannot buy a position, remove a
criticism, or have a competitor removed. Apps with no affiliate programme are
reviewed and ranked exactly like the others.

## How to spot them

Affiliate links are marked with a `sponsored` attribute in the page source and
always sit next to a disclosure notice.
