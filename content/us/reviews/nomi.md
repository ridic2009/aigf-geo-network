## Memory is the product

Most apps in this category claim long-term memory. Nomi is the one where we could
verify it: after two weeks it referred back to a detail from the first session
without being prompted, and it did so in context rather than as a canned callback.

## Conversation

Nomi follows rather than leads. Replies are shorter than Candy AI's and much less
likely to escalate a topic on their own. Whether that is better depends entirely
on what you want from the app.

## Where it falls behind

Image generation exists but is not competitive, and the price is the highest of
the three apps we ranked. If media features matter to you, read our
[Candy AI review](/reviews/candy-ai/).
