## Who we are

AI Girlfriend Ranking is an independent review site covering AI companion apps.
The same small editorial team tests every app on every market we publish in, so a
score means the same thing on all of our sites.

## How we test

Every app is used on a paid account for at least two weeks before it is scored.
We score five criteria — conversation, memory, media, pricing and account
controls — and re-verify the score before each monthly update. Scores are set
before any commercial conversation with a vendor.

## How we make money

Some links on this site are affiliate links. If you sign up through one, we may
earn a commission at no extra cost to you. This never changes a rating or a
ranking position. See our [affiliate disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/).

## Corrections

If something on this site is wrong or out of date, write to us and we will fix
it and date the correction.
