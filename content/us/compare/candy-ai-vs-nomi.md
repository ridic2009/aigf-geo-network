## The short answer

Pick **Candy AI** if you want one subscription that covers chat, images and
voice. Pick **Nomi** if conversation quality and memory are the whole point and
you do not care about generated images.

## Conversation

Nomi wins. It holds a thread longer, stays in character more consistently, and
follows your lead instead of steering. Candy AI is fluent but more assertive.

## Media

Candy AI wins, and not narrowly. Its image generation keeps the character
consistent between pictures, which is the single hardest thing in this category.

## Price and value

Candy AI is cheaper and covers more features per dollar. Nomi charges more for a
narrower product that happens to be the best at what it does.
