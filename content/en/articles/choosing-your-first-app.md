Most buying advice in this category is a list of apps with adjectives attached.
That is useless, because the apps differ less than the reasons people open them.
These five questions do the actual narrowing.

## 1. What do you want it to do when you are bored?

This is the fork that decides everything else.

If the answer is **"talk to me like it knows me,"** you want continuity: memory
across sessions, a consistent voice, a character that does not reset. That is
the lane [Nomi](/reviews/nomi-review/) is built for.

If the answer is **"show me something,"** you want media: image generation and
voice inside the same subscription rather than sold as credits.
[Candy AI](/reviews/candy-ai-review/) covers that ground, which is why it leads
[our ranking](/best-ai-girlfriend-apps/) — not because it wins every category,
but because it is the only one that does all three without a second purchase.

If the answer is **"tell me a story,"** you want roleplay and scene quality, and
continuity matters much less than you would think.

Picking the wrong lane is the single most common reason people conclude the
whole category is disappointing.

## 2. How much does the filter matter to you?

Content policy is the least discussed and most decisive variable. Some apps
refuse a great deal; some refuse almost nothing. Neither is better in the
abstract, but discovering the answer three days in is a waste of a month's
subscription.

If unfiltered conversation is the point, check it in the free tier before paying
— that is the ground [Secret Desires](/reviews/secret-desires-review/) and
[Joi](/reviews/joi-review/) compete on. If it is not the point, a permissive app
may simply feel cheaper than you want.

## 3. Where will you actually use it?

Half the apps in this category are web-only. Among the ones we cover,
[Camsoda AI](/reviews/camsoda-ai-review/) and Secret Desires run in a browser,
while Candy AI, Nomi, [Kupid](/reviews/kupid-ai-review/),
[Sweetdream](/reviews/sweetdream-review/) and
[Replika](/reviews/replika-review/) have native apps on iOS and Android.

If you expect to use it in bed on a phone, a mobile browser session that logs
you out weekly will end the habit faster than any shortcoming in the writing.

## 4. What will it cost in month three?

Not month one. The intro discount is not the product. Among the apps we track,
published monthly prices run from $10.00 for Sweetdream to $19.99 for Replika,
with Candy AI at $12.99 and Nomi at $15.99 — but the headline is only half the
question, because credits for images and voice sit on top of it in several apps.

This deserves its own checklist, which is
[what these apps really cost](/articles/ai-girlfriend-app-prices/).

## 5. How do you get out?

Before you subscribe, find three things: where the cancel button is, whether
there is a data export, and whether account deletion is self-service or a
support ticket. An app that hides all three is telling you how it plans to treat
you. We cover the specifics in
[deleting an AI companion account](/articles/deleting-an-ai-companion-account/).

## A workable first month

Pick two apps from the lane you chose in question one. Use both free tiers for
three days, judging only the conversation. Pay for one month of the better one.
Then re-read question five before the renewal date rather than after it.

That sequence costs about $13 and answers more than any amount of reading,
including this.
