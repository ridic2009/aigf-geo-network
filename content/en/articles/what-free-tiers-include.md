All eight apps we track have a free tier. That fact is close to meaningless on
its own, because "free" covers two completely different products.

## The two kinds of free

**A capped product.** You can use it indefinitely. Text chat works, memory
works, and the limits sit on media and on how fast you can send messages. You
could stay here for months and have a real sense of the app.

**A trailer.** A handful of messages or a few days, then a wall. It exists to
show you the interface, not the product. Anything you conclude from it about
memory or consistency is guesswork, because neither shows up in a day.

Telling them apart takes about ten minutes: use it, hit something, and read what
the wall says. A message about a daily allowance means the first kind. A message
about upgrading to continue means the second.

## What gets metered, and why

The pattern is consistent across the category because the underlying costs are:

- **Images** cost the operator real money each time. Always capped, usually
  hard.
- **Voice** is metered by the minute or the message. Often the first thing to
  disappear.
- **Video**, where offered at all, is paid-only in practice.
- **Message volume** is capped by count or by rate, but generously compared to
  media.
- **Memory depth** is sometimes shorter on free plans, which makes a free tier
  a poor way to judge exactly the feature that matters most.

That last point is the trap. Judging an app's memory from its free tier can tell
you it is worse than it is.

## Which free tiers are worth the time

Among the apps we test, [Sweetdream](/reviews/sweetdream-review/) is the one
whose free tier reads as a product rather than a demo — it is the reason it sits
second in [our ranking](/best-ai-girlfriend-apps/) at a 4.5 despite a $10.00
paid plan that is the cheapest of the group.

[Replika](/reviews/replika-review/) is the other one worth an unpaid fortnight,
for a different reason: the free experience is the classic version of this
category, and if it leaves you cold, the more expensive apps are unlikely to
change your mind.

[Kupid AI](/reviews/kupid-ai-review/) lists free chat immediately after sign-up,
which makes it a cheap way to test whether its character builder suits you.

## How to use a free tier properly

Spend the first session on conversation alone. Do not generate a single image —
you will exhaust the cap and learn nothing about the writing.

Give it one real fact about yourself on day one and ask about it on day three.
That single test tells you more than the entire features list.

Then, and only then, spend the media allowance in one sitting, so you see the
quality at its best rather than one picture a day.
