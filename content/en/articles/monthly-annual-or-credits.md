Every app in this category sells one of three ways, and each is built around a
different guess about you.

## Monthly: priced for people who might leave

The highest per-month cost and the lowest risk. You are paying a premium for the
option to stop, and in a market where the product behind the subscription can be
retuned without notice, that option has real value.

**Right for:** anyone in their first three months with an app, and anyone whose
usage comes in bursts.

## Annual: priced for people who already stayed

Typically 30–50% cheaper per month. The catch is not the commitment itself, it
is what you are committing to. Models get replaced, content filters get retuned,
and a companion that suited you in spring can read differently by autumn. None
of that voids an annual plan.

**Right for:** month four onwards, on an app you have actually used daily.

A useful test before clicking: if this app changed its model tomorrow and the
character felt 20% different, would you still want the remaining eleven months?
If the honest answer is no, pay monthly.

## Credits: priced for people who underestimate themselves

Credits meter the expensive things — images, voice minutes, video. They look
cheap because the entry bundle is small, and they are sold in tiers where the
per-unit price falls as the bundle grows, which is a structure designed to move
you up the ladder.

Two things to check that are almost never on the purchase screen: whether unused
credits expire, and whether a subscription includes an allowance that resets. An
app that includes media in the plan removes this entire decision — which is the
practical argument for [Candy AI](/reviews/candy-ai-review/) over a cheaper
subscription with a credit meter attached.

**Right for:** people who genuinely use media occasionally. Nobody else.

## The sequence that loses least

1. **Free tier, three days.** Judge the conversation and nothing else. Some
   apps, like [Sweetdream](/reviews/sweetdream-review/) at 4.5 in
   [our ranking](/best-ai-girlfriend-apps/), are worth a full unpaid fortnight
   here.
2. **One month, monthly billing.** Now judge memory, repetition and the media
   quality.
3. **Decide at the renewal, not before it.** If you did not open the app on more
   than half the days, stop.
4. **Annual only from month four**, and only on an app that survived step three
   twice.

## The one number worth writing down

Cost per day you actually opened it. A $15.99 plan used daily is 53 cents a day.
The same plan opened four times in a month is $4 a visit, and no discount code
fixes that ratio.

Track it for one month. It settles the annual-plan question more decisively than
any comparison, including [ours](/articles/ai-girlfriend-app-prices/).
