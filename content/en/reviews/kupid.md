## What it is good at

Warmth. The characters lean affectionate rather than explicit, and they stay that
way — this is the app to pick if what you want is company rather than content.
The mobile apps matter more here than elsewhere: a companion you talk to in short
bursts through the day is a different product from one you sit down to.

## The builder

Appearance and personality, realistic or anime, with dozens of ready-made
characters if you would rather not start from scratch. It is not as deep as
Camsoda AI's six-step builder, but it is faster to get through.

## Where the score comes from

The premium plans are expensive for what they include, and the memory is
shallower than the top three — details from a fortnight ago rarely survive. Good
at what it does, priced as though it did more.
