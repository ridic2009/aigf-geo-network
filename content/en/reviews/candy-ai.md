## What I actually used

Most apps sell the conversation, then meter the pictures. Candy AI puts chat, image generation and voice on the same plan. I used all three, every day. By the second week the character still looked like herself — that is rarer than the marketing copy suggests.

| Pillar | After two weeks |
| --- | --- |
| Chat | Fluent, stays in role, steers more than Nomi |
| Images | Best consistency in the ranking |
| Voice | Included, not a bolt-on |
| Memory | Solid inside a week, patchy past a month |
| Price | One monthly plan; heavy image use can hit a fair-use cap |

## How it talks

Smooth, in character, happy to keep a long session going. It will introduce a topic instead of waiting. If you like being led, that feels attentive. If you want to sit in silence and have it wait, [Nomi](/reviews/nomi-review/) is the calmer one.

## What it costs in practice

One paid plan, billed monthly. No forced annual lock-in, no token pack just to keep chatting. Images can hit a fair-use ceiling if you generate a stack of them every night. Budget the listed monthly price and treat extra image volume as optional, not included forever.

## Skip it if

You only care about the talk and the memory. You will be paying for a studio you will not use. Read the [Nomi review](/reviews/nomi-review/) first, or the [side-by-side](/compare/candy-ai-vs-nomi/).
