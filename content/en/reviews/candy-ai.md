## What you get

Candy AI bundles three things most competitors sell separately: a text chat with
a character that persists, image generation tied to that character, and voice
replies. After two weeks of daily use the image feature was the strongest part —
the characters stayed recognisable from one picture to the next, which is the
hardest thing to get right in this category.

## Conversation quality

The dialogue runs smoothly and stays in role. Long sessions hold up well, though
the app steers the conversation more readily than Nomi does — it will introduce
a topic rather than wait for you.

## What it actually costs

One paid plan, billed monthly. No forced annual subscription and no hidden
credit system for ordinary chat, although heavy image use can reach a fair-use
limit. Budget for the standard plan and treat anything else as optional.

## Who it is not for

If only the conversation and the memory matter to you, you are paying for
features you will not use. Read our [Nomi review](/reviews/nomi-review/) first.
