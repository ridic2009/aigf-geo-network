## The memory is the product

Nearly every app in this category claims long-term memory. Nomi is the one where
we could verify it: after two weeks, a detail from the first session came back on
its own, in the right context, without being prompted.

## Conversation

Nomi follows rather than leads. Replies are shorter than Candy AI's and it starts
a new topic far less often on its own initiative. Whether that reads as restraint
or as passivity depends on what you want from the app — for a companion you talk
*with*, it is the better behaviour.

## Where it drops off

Image generation exists but is not competitive, and the price is the highest of
the apps we rank. If media matters to you, read our
[Candy AI review](/reviews/candy-ai-review/) instead.
