## What the price buys

Uncensored chat, a list of ready-made characters, and not much else. For some
readers that is exactly the product they want, and it would be dishonest to mark
an app down for being what it says it is.

## Where it runs out

Replies are short and rarely develop a scene on their own — you carry the
conversation. Memory between sessions is minimal. There is no character builder
worth the name, and the media features that the rest of this ranking treats as
standard are absent.

## What to pick instead

If the low price is the appeal but you want the app to hold up its end,
[Secret Desires](/reviews/secret-desires-review/) is the step up. If you want the
cheapest way to find out whether the category is for you at all, use
[Sweetdream](/reviews/sweetdream-review/)'s free tier and pay nothing.
