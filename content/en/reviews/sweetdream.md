## Where it stands out

Most apps in this category open with a question. Sweetdream opens with a scene —
it leads with a situation and lets you answer into it. Over two weeks that made
it the most pleasant one to come back to, even though it is not the most capable
on paper.

## The free tier

Worth singling out, because in this category "free" usually means a demo that
runs out in twenty messages. Here the conversation stays open and the limits fall
on the media instead. If you want to find out whether an AI companion is for you
before paying anyone, start here.

## Where it falls behind

The memory is shorter than Nomi's — details from a week back come and go — and
the character builder is thin next to Camsoda AI's. In long sessions it repeats
phrasings more than the top three do.
