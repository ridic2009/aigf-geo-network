## The intro price is not the price

Most apps in this category advertise a first-month rate. The number you should
budget for is the renewal rate, which is often double. Check the renewal price
before you enter a card, not after — it is usually in the small print under the
plan selector rather than on the plan itself.

## Tokens on top of the subscription

The subscription buys the conversation. Images, voice minutes and video are
frequently metered separately, and this is where a $12 plan turns into a $30
month. Candy AI includes more of it in the base plan than most; apps that sell
tokens separately are not necessarily worse value, but you cannot compare them by
the subscription price alone.

## Annual plans and what they cost you

An annual plan at half the monthly rate is a good deal only if the app is still
the one you want in six months, in a category that changes every quarter. We
would not lock in for a year on anything here yet.

## Where the free tiers end

Free tiers in this category almost always limit media first and conversation
second — you will hit the image cap long before the message cap. That makes them
useful for judging how an app talks and useless for judging what it can show you.

## How to work out your real number

1. Find the renewal price, not the first-month price.
2. Check whether images and voice come out of the subscription or out of tokens.
3. Use the free tier for a week and note what you actually used.
4. Multiply what you used by the metered rate, and add the renewal price.

That figure is what the app costs you. In our testing it lands between $10 and
$20 a month for ordinary use, and above $30 for anyone who leans on the image
features daily.
