## What we collect

This site is static files. No accounts, no comments, no forms. No advertising cookies. We do not sell data.

## Server logs

The host keeps standard access logs (IP, user agent, URL, timestamp) for security and breakage. They are kept for a limited time and are not used to build profiles.

## Outbound links

When you leave for a provider, their privacy policy applies. Some links include a campaign parameter so they can see the referral.
