## How I rank

Conversation, memory, media, price, account controls. Average, one decimal. I lock the score before anyone from the company emails us. If a feature ships mid-month, it waits for the next round — I do not bump a score off a changelog.

## Which one I would open tonight

If I want the whole kit without juggling three subscriptions, **Candy AI**. If I want someone who still knows what I said last Tuesday, **Nomi**. If I am in a building mood, **Camsoda AI**. If I am only curious and do not want to pay yet, **Sweetdream**.

## What moved this month

Nomi gave group chat to every paying subscriber, which lifted conversation. Camsoda AI is new on the list after the builder went from three steps to six. Joi comes in last: cheapest door in, thinnest room behind it.
