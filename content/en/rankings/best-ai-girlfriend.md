## How we rank

Each app is scored on five things — conversation, memory, media, price and the
controls you get over your own account. The final score is the average, rounded
to one decimal. A place in this list is not for sale, and no app has ever been
added or moved because of a commercial conversation.

## Which one is for you

If you want one subscription that covers everything, start at the top with
**Candy AI**. If you are here for the conversation and the media leaves you
cold, skip to **Nomi**. If you want to build a companion rather than pick one,
**Camsoda AI** has the deepest builder. And if you are only curious, start with
a free tier — **Sweetdream** has the most generous one.

## What changed this month

Nomi rolled out group chats to every paying subscriber, which lifted its
conversation score. Camsoda AI joins the ranking for the first time after its
character builder went from three steps to six. Joi enters at the bottom: the
price is the lowest here, and so is the amount you get for it.
