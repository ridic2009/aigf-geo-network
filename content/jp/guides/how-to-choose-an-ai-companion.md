## Start with the free tier

Every app in this category has one, and it tells you more than the marketing page
does. Spend an evening on it before entering card details.

## Check the memory claim

Tell the app something specific and unusual. Come back three days later and see
whether it comes up naturally.

## Read the pricing page twice

Look for credit systems, image quotas and annual-only discounts. The advertised
monthly price is often not what you end up paying.
